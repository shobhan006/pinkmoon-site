WE ARE A STORYSTUDIO

A story can start as a film. It can become a conversation, a character, a community, a format, a fandom or something nobody planned for. That's the space we're interested in. We find ideas in culture, turn them into content and build the world around them so people can see themselves in it, talk about it and make it their own.

CULTURE

Culture is everywhere, in music, fashion, technology, the internet, food, language, relationships and the way we live. We're interested in all of it because that's where the interesting stories usually begin.

COMMUNITY

Community isn't about gathering an audience around something. It's about creating something people recognise themselves in, and want to come back to because it feels a little like theirs.

CONTENT

We shape stories for the world. Films, series, formats, IP, branded entertainment, digital and new forms of storytelling. The format can change, the platform can change. What doesn't change is the idea at the centre of it. We start there and build outwards.

Kanishak Rajpal

Founder & Creative Director

Kanishak is a creative and communications leader with 13+ years of building brands, content ecosystems and digital narratives across media, entertainment, FMCG, technology and Web3. At PINKMOON he sets the creative direction, shaping the ideas, the stories and the systems that carry them across culture, content and community.

He believes creativity should live inside culture, not interrupt it. Lately his focus is AI-led storytelling, using AI to scale ideas faster without losing the human thinking behind them. If you think creativity should solve real problems and still feel human, you'll probably get along.

Shobhan Sharma

Co-Founder & Filmmaker

Shobhan is a filmmaker who builds stories for the screens people actually live on. At PINKMOON he leads the making, taking an idea from a single spark to a finished series and shaping the look, the performances and the rhythm of every frame.

His work lives in short-form episodic storytelling: sitcoms, micro dramas, branded entertainment and original IP. Every project starts with a character, a world and a feeling. When he isn't on set or in the edit, he's writing the next episode.
